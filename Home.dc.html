About

Hi, I'm Nat — a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Political Science at Columbia University. My work sits at the intersection of comparative politics and political behavior, with a focus on how democratic institutions bend and hold under pressure. I care a lot about doing careful, transparent empirical work that speaks to people outside the seminar room too.

Before Columbia, I studied government and statistics at Dartmouth College. When I'm not staring at survey data, I'm usually running, cooking something involving too much garlic, or trying to keep up with the news out of places I study. Feel free to reach out — I love talking about this stuff.
